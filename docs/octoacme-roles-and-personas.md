# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define testing strategies and manage quality assurance throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and test plans aligned with project scope
- Review acceptance criteria with Product Manager and Developers
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Manage test case library and maintain test coverage metrics
- Validate feature acceptance before handoff to release
- Triage defects and work with Developers on resolution

### Goals
- Ensure product meets quality standards
- Reduce production defects
- Maintain test coverage metrics

### Interactions
- Works with Developers to understand implementation and design test cases
- Partners with Product Manager on acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Ensures features pass quality gate before moving to release
- Coordinates with DevOps on QA environment setup

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and QA strategy reviews
- Defect reports and test coverage updates
- Quality gate approvals before release

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance and oversee technical quality of delivery. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical approach and architectural decisions
- Conduct design reviews and architecture validation
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and share technical knowledge
- Ensure code quality and architectural standards

### Goals
- Maintain system health and scalability
- Reduce technical debt over time
- Guide technically sound decisions

### Interactions
- Partners with Developers on design and code reviews
- Advises Project Manager on technical risks and timeline implications
- Collaborates with Security on architectural security requirements
- Reviews technical approach for feasibility

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture discussions
- Technical guidance in PR reviews
- Risk assessments and mitigation planning

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers review security and regulatory requirements, conduct security reviews, and ensure compliance with organizational policies. They manage incident response escalation and maintain security posture.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements in acceptance criteria
- Conduct security reviews and vulnerability assessments
- Ensure compliance with organizational policies and standards
- Manage incident response escalation process
- Advise on data protection and privacy requirements

### Goals
- Protect systems and data from security vulnerabilities
- Maintain compliance posture
- Minimize security risk exposure

### Interactions
- Reviews acceptance criteria for security implications
- Participates in risk assessment and mitigation planning
- Coordinates with incident response team
- Partners with Technical Lead on architectural security design

### Typical Communication
- Security review participation and sign-offs
- Incident response coordination
- Policy and compliance updates

---

## DevOps / Deployment Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Deployment Engineers design deployment pipelines, manage infrastructure, execute releases, and monitor production systems. They enable reliable, automated deployments and maintain system uptime.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD infrastructure
- Execute releases following Release & Deployment guide
- Monitor production systems and maintain observability
- Assist with rollback and incident response procedures
- Coordinate infrastructure setup for testing environments

### Goals
- Enable reliable, automated deployments
- Maintain system uptime and observability
- Reduce deployment risk and cycle time

### Interactions
- Partners with Developers on CI/CD setup and pipeline design
- Follows Release & Deployment guide for release execution
- Assists with rollback and incident response when needed
- Coordinates with QA on test environment setup

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and execution updates
- Infrastructure and pipeline status reports
- Incident response coordination

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach (optional)

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile team practices, remove impediments, and help teams optimize their workflow. They coach teams on agile principles and track delivery metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate team ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives)
- Remove blockers and impediments to team progress
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track velocity and delivery metrics
- Help team optimize workflow and reduce cycle time

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and delivery predictability
- Increase team autonomy and ownership
- Foster psychological safety and collaboration

### Interactions
- Facilitates standups and retrospectives with all team members
- Supports Product Manager and Project Manager in planning
- Works with Project Manager to manage risks and dependencies
- Helps team identify and resolve process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of team ceremonies
- Velocity tracking and metrics reporting
- Continuous improvement recommendations

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Engineer (optional)

### Role Summary
Business Analysts and Requirements Engineers gather business requirements, document acceptance criteria, and analyze the impact of changes. They help ensure requirements clarity and scope management.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Refine acceptance criteria with Product Manager and stakeholders
- Analyze scope and impact of proposed changes
- Support QA with detailed test scenarios
- Manage requirements traceability and documentation

### Goals
- Ensure requirements clarity and completeness
- Reduce rework due to ambiguous requirements
- Improve stakeholder alignment

### Interactions
- Works with Product Manager to refine and prioritize requirements
- Partners with Project Manager on scope management
- Supports QA with detailed test scenarios and acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with Developers on requirements clarification

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering and documentation
- Scope change impact analysis
- Test scenario and acceptance criteria review

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When working on projects, reference the appropriate personas to clarify roles, responsibilities, and interaction patterns for your team.
